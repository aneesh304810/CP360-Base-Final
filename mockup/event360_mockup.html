The envelope

Six fields. Every event instantiates 3 or 4 of them — nothing else is ever on the wire.

Type, length and nullability are NOT_SPECIFIED — by design. The specification never states them. The catalogue shows the spec does not say rather than an empty cell, because an empty cell invites someone to fill it in. Inferring VARCHAR2(50) here would publish a contract SEI never wrote.
